# DBS Bank

DBS Bank, originally the Development Bank of Singapore, is a Singaporean multinational banking and financial services corporation and the largest bank in Singapore by assets. It was founded in 1968 and is headquartered in Singapore. Its largest shareholder is Temasek Holdings, the Singapore state investment company.

DBS is known for its early and sustained investment in digital banking, and has been repeatedly named the world's best digital bank by financial publications. The bank serves consumer, small business, and corporate customers across Singapore, Hong Kong, China, India, Indonesia, and Taiwan.

DBS offers a full range of services including retail and wealth management, corporate and institutional banking, treasury and markets, and increasingly digital and API-based banking services. It has positioned itself around technology-led banking, including artificial intelligence and data-driven personalisation.

DBS is one of the highest-rated banks in Asia and a constituent of the Straits Times Index, Singapore's benchmark stock index.
