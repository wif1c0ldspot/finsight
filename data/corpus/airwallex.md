# Airwallex

Airwallex is a global financial technology company that provides cross-border payments and financial infrastructure for businesses. It was founded in 2015 in Melbourne, Australia, by Jack Zhang, Max Li, Lucy Liu, and Xijing Dai. The company's global headquarters is located in Singapore.

Airwallex's core product is a cross-border payments platform that lets businesses collect, hold, and move money internationally. Its offerings include multi-currency business accounts, corporate cards, foreign exchange at interbank rates, and embedded finance APIs that other companies can integrate into their own products.

The company raised its Series E funding round in 2022, reaching a valuation of approximately 5.6 billion US dollars. Investors have included Tencent, Sequoia Capital China, and Mastercard. Airwallex processes tens of billions of dollars in annual transaction volume and serves customers ranging from small online sellers to large enterprises.

Airwallex competes primarily with established cross-border payment providers and neobanks, and has expanded its presence across Asia Pacific, Europe, and North America.
