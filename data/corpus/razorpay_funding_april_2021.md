# Razorpay: April 2021 Series E

On 19 April 2021, Razorpay announced US$160 million of Series E funding at a US$3 billion valuation. The post named GIC and Sequoia as co-leaders and mentioned Ribbit Capital and Matrix Partners. The company described making online payments accessible to businesses as its original objective. These figures concern the April round, not the later December 2021 financing.
