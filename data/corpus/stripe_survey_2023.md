# Stripe: March 2023 business survey

Stripe published a business survey on 28 March 2023 covering 2,500 business leaders in nine countries. In that survey, 65% were confident about their own business growth while 80% were pessimistic about the economy. Inflation was the leading concern, and 72% reported higher operating costs than a year earlier. These percentages describe surveyed businesses, not Stripe financial results or the entire economy.
