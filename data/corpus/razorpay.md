# Razorpay: December 2021 Series F

Razorpay announced an approximately US$375 million Series F on 20 December 2021 at a US$7.5 billion valuation. Lone Pine Capital, Alkeon Capital and TCV co-led the round. The release stated that total investment since its 2014 inception had reached US$740 million. It identified expansion of RazorpayX business banking and acquisitions of B2B software companies among its plans.
