# Razorpay

Razorpay is an Indian financial technology company that provides payment processing solutions for businesses in India. It was founded in 2014 by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar, and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India. The company was part of the Y Combinator accelerator program.

Razorpay's core product is a payment gateway that lets Indian merchants accept online payments via cards, net banking, UPI, and popular wallets. It has since expanded into a full-stack payments and banking platform, offering products such as RazorpayX for business banking, payment links, subscriptions, and payroll.

In 2021, Razorpay reached a valuation of approximately 7.5 billion US dollars following its Series F funding round. It is one of the largest and most valuable private fintech companies in India, competing with domestic and international payment providers.

Razorpay primarily serves small and medium-sized businesses and startups across India, and has grown rapidly alongside the country's digital payments boom.
