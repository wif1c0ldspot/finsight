# Airwallex: Series F snapshot

In a 21 May 2025 announcement, Airwallex reported a Series F valuation of US$6.2 billion and cumulative funding of US$1.2 billion. It said the capital would support expansion into new markets. The company described its founding mission in 2015 as making cross-border money movement faster, cheaper and more transparent. Valuation and cumulative funding are different measures.
