# DBS: 2023 results briefing

The 7 February 2024 DBS results briefing reported 2023 total income of SGD20.2 billion and net profit of approximately SGD10.3 billion before two one-time items. Including those items, net profit was approximately SGD10.1 billion. The one-time items were a SGD100 million social-responsibility contribution and SGD124 million of Citi Taiwan integration costs. Return on equity was 18%. Rounded profit figures should not be treated as exact amounts.
