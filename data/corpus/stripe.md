# Stripe: February 2024 employee tender offer

On 28 February 2024, Stripe announced agreements for an employee tender offer valuing the company at US$65 billion, also reported as EUR60 billion. The transaction provided liquidity to current and former employees. Investors supplied most of the funds; Stripe also planned share repurchases using some of its own capital to offset employee-equity dilution. The valuation refers to this transaction, not a current stock-market price.
