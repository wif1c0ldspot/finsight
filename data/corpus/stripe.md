# Stripe

Stripe is an American-Irish financial technology company that builds payment processing software and application programming interfaces for e-commerce websites and mobile applications. It was founded in 2010 by brothers Patrick Collison and John Collison, and is dual-headquartered in South San Francisco, California, and Dublin, Ireland.

Stripe's core product is a payments API that lets developers accept online payments with a few lines of code. The company has since expanded into a suite of financial products including Stripe Connect for marketplaces, Stripe Billing for subscriptions, Stripe Atlas for company formation, and Stripe Treasury for banking-as-a-service.

Stripe reached a peak private valuation of 95 billion US dollars in 2021. In 2024, following a secondary share sale, its valuation was reported at approximately 65 billion US dollars. The company processes hundreds of billions of dollars in annual payment volume for millions of businesses.

Stripe is widely considered the leading payments infrastructure company for internet businesses, and its developer-first approach is often credited with lowering the barrier to online payments.
