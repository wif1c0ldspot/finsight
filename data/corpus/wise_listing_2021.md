# Wise: July 2021 direct listing

In a 7 July 2021 post, Wise said it would complete a direct listing on the London Stock Exchange that day. It described OwnWise as a programme involving customers as shareholders and reported more than 125,000 pre-applications. The post described Wise as having developed from a money-transfer service into an international account. A pre-application count is not a count of completed share purchases.
